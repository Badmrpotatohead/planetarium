CREDITS

open source, MIT license · Esc to close

Planet and Moon maps

Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune and the Moon by Solar System Scope, based on NASA mission data. Licensed CC BY 4.0; used unmodified.

Earth maps

Day, night lights, clouds, normal and specular maps from the three.js example textures (MIT License).

Orbits

J2000 mean orbital elements from NASA JPL Solar System Dynamics; moon, dwarf planet and spacecraft figures from NASA fact sheets.

Software and type

three.js r160 (MIT). Plus Jakarta Sans and JetBrains Mono via Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License).

Drawn in code

The Sun, dwarf planets, small moons, spacecraft, belts, comet, Milky Way and galaxy have no image assets. Offline, every world falls back to a procedural surface painted at load.
